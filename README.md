
# ⚒️ AnvilWake Studios


Welcome to AnvilWake Studios!

I'm an independent developer working on games, server projects, development tools, and whatever else catches my interest along the way.

AnvilWake started as a way to bring my projects together under one name. Since then, it's grown into something more: a place where I can document my work, share what I'm learning, and build a community around game development.

I'm not running a big studio with a team of professionals. Most of this starts with an idea, a lot of research, some trial and error, and probably a few things breaking before they work.

That's part of the fun.

## 🎮 Projects in the Forge

### 🌊 Forge&Tide
**Status: Active Development — Phase 2 of 6**

An original fantasy roster RPG being developed for PC and mobile.

The project focuses on strategic turn-based combat, character collection, team composition, and an original fantasy world.

Current development areas include:
- Speed-based combat and turn-meter systems.
- Character abilities, roles, and progression.
- Factions, regions, and worldbuilding.
- Team-building and tactical gameplay.

This is my primary development project.

### ⚔️ ForgeDFO
**Status: Side Project**

A custom Dungeon Fighter Online server development project.

My work involves exploring server systems, troubleshooting, experimenting with gameplay features, and learning more about the game's underlying architecture.

Development happens when time and interest allow.

### 🍁 ForgeMS
**Status: Inactive — Future Revival & Rebranding**

ForgeMS began as a custom MapleStory v83 server project.

Active development has been discontinued for now, but I intend to revisit it in the future with a new direction and branding.

It's one of the projects that helped push me further into game development.

### 🤖 ForgeConstruct
**Status: Planning**

A modular Discord bot project intended to support AnvilWake's community and development activities.

Planned areas include:
- Community management and moderation.
- Discord automation.
- Developer utilities.
- Project notifications.
- Modular commands and features.

I'm currently researching open-source bot architectures and planning the initial development structure.

### ⚰️ Forge & Horde
**Status: Early Planning**

Another original game concept currently in the planning stages.

More information will be shared as the project takes shape.

## 💻 Development & Learning

I work across different areas of development, depending on what a project needs.

That includes game systems, server development, programming, debugging, automation, and documentation.

I don't claim to know everything. A big part of AnvilWake is learning how things work, figuring out why they don't work, and improving them.

Some projects are experimental. Others are long-term goals.

Not everything here will be finished overnight, and not every repository will contain a playable game.

I'll share progress as there's something worth showing.

## 🔥 The AnvilWake Community

Alongside development, I've opened an AnvilWake Studios Discord.

It's a place for developers, programmers, gamers, testers, writers, and other creative people to hang out and share what they're working on.

We're building up:

- Development discussions and project updates.
